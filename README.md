# Customer Trends Data Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing customer shopping behavior to understand purchasing patterns, customer preferences, and sales performance.

The main goal is to use Python, SQL, and Power BI together to explore customer data, answer business questions, and identify useful insights that can help businesses make better decisions.

The project follows three main stages:

- **Data Preparation and Exploratory Data Analysis (Python):** Cleaned and prepared the dataset, handled missing values, created new columns, and explored customer shopping patterns using Pandas.
- **Data Analysis (SQL):** Used MySQL queries to analyze customer segments, purchasing behavior, product performance, discounts, and subscription patterns.
- **Dashboard and Insights (Power BI):** Created an interactive dashboard to present important metrics, sales trends, and customer behavior through charts and filters.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Understand customer shopping and purchasing patterns.
- Analyze sales and revenue across different product categories.
- Compare the shopping behavior of subscribers and non-subscribers.
- Identify customer segments based on purchasing frequency.
- Understand discount usage and customer spending behavior.
- Analyze customer preferences and product performance.
- Present meaningful business insights through an interactive dashboard.

## 📊 Dataset Information

The dataset contains **3,900 purchase records** and **18 columns**.

It includes information such as:

- Customer details
- Age and gender
- Product categories
- Purchase amount
- Review ratings
- Discounts and subscriptions
- Shipping types
- Previous purchases
- Purchase frequency

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Python** – Data cleaning and exploratory data analysis.
- **Pandas** – Data manipulation and analysis.
- **Jupyter Notebook** – Writing and executing Python code.
- **MySQL** – Database management and SQL analysis.
- **Power BI** – Dashboard creation and data visualization.

## 💡 Key Insights

Based on the analysis and Power BI dashboard, the following insights were identified:

- **Clothing was the top-performing category**, generating the highest sales and revenue among all product categories.
- **Outerwear had the lowest sales and revenue** compared to the other categories.
- **Young Adult customers contributed the highest sales and revenue** among all age groups.
- **Non-subscribers represented 73% of customers**, while subscribers accounted for 27%.
- The average purchase amount was **$59.76**, and the average review rating was **3.75**.
